# OpenMAIC Teach product context

## Register

`product`. This is a private learning environment, not a marketing site, a
clinical decision-support tool, or a credentialing product.

## Audience and purpose

The current clinician track serves practicing adult acute/ICU respiratory
therapists. It helps learners explain what supplied bedside evidence supports,
separate observations, settings, measurements, calculations, and models, and
identify a justified assessment, reassessment, or communication need.

The intended learner feeling is: “I understand why that distinction matters, I
can explain it, and I know what information would change my interpretation.”
Screens should make a usable relationship visible, show its boundary, and then
support an honest application. They should not become narrated chapters,
generic dashboards, or patient-specific simulators.

## Track boundary

The clinician-track prototype is adult acute/ICU RT only. It does not define a
shared experience for pediatric, neonatal, obstetric, or other future tracks;
those tracks require their own scope, source mapping, and review.

## Current prototype context

The standalone `/m02-prototype` is a limited Teaching Case A → paired-breath
model → Teaching Case A slice. It is ungraded, has no provider interaction, and
does not establish clinical readiness, educational effectiveness, or a
patient-specific conclusion.

M02 teaches that oxygenation, CO2 elimination, and breathing effort are related
but distinct questions. The prototype uses a reviewed fixed teaching model and
keeps that model separate from the fictional case evidence.
