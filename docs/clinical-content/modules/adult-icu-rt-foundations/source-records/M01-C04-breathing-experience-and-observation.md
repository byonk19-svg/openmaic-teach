## Clinically consequential claim

In B2, an awake patient’s report that breathing feels difficult and visible inspiratory neck-muscle recruitment are meaningful qualitative assessment evidence. They support a concern to assess and characterize; they do not prove the cause, severity, or quantified respiratory-muscle work.

DOI: [10.1513/AnnalsATS.202301-023ST](https://doi.org/10.1513/AnnalsATS.202301-023ST). ATS approval December 2022; publication 2023-04; accessed 2026-09-16.
