## Clinically consequential claim

A valid calculated exhaled minute-volume result alone does not establish adequate CO2 clearance. Relevant current CO2/gas information needs interpretation with time and support context.

## Evidence

- Wang MC, Corbridge TC, McCrimmon DR, Walter JM. *Teaching an intuitive derivation of the clinical alveolar equations: mass balance as a fundamental physiological principle.* Advances in Physiology Education. 2020;44(2):145-152. DOI: [10.1152/advan.00064.2019](https://doi.org/10.1152/advan.00064.2019). Version/date: 2020 article of record; accessed 2026-09-16.
  - Exact location: section/derivation headed **Alveolar ventilation**, Equation 1 (`V̇A = RR × VT × (1 − VD/VT)`) and its CO2 mass-balance derivation.
  - Permitted paraphrase: total minute volume and alveolar ventilation differ when dead-space fraction is nonzero; CO2 interpretation relies on the alveolar-ventilation/CO2 relationship, not total exhaled volume alone.
- Haouzi P. *Last Word on Viewpoint: Revisiting the effects of the reciprocal function between alveolar ventilation and CO2 partial pressure (PACO2) on PACO2 homeostasis at rest and in exercise.* Journal of Applied Physiology. 2022;133(4):918.   - Exact location: paragraph discussing factors that affect pulmonary CO2 exchange, including acute changes in mechanical ventilatory support.
  - Permitted paraphrase: support context and CO2-exchange conditions matter when interpreting CO2 physiology.
