## Clinically consequential claim

A patient-ventilator assessment is a comprehensive clinician assessment for a patient receiving noninvasive or invasive mechanical ventilation; it includes physical examination, bedside physiologic data, airway assessment, ventilator settings and monitoring, humidification, and documentation. Its focus is the patient rather than the ventilator.
