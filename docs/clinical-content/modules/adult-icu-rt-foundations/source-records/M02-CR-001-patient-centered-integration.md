## Clinically consequential claim

Oxygenation evidence, CO2 evidence, total gas moved, support conditions, patient report, and bedside observations are related but noninterchangeable inputs to a patient-centered respiratory assessment. A useful value on one pathway does not erase meaningful evidence on another.
