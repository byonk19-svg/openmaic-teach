## Clinically consequential claim

SpO2 is a pulse-oximetric observation relevant to arterial oxygenation, not a complete description of oxygen delivery or respiratory state. Inspired-oxygen/support context materially affects its interpretation; the same SpO2 under different FiO2 conditions is not automatically equivalent context. Hemoglobin/oxygen content and perfusion may be named as downstream oxygen-pathway questions without calculating oxygen delivery.

## Evidence

- Wick KD, Matthay MA, Ware LB. *Pulse oximetry for the diagnosis and management of acute respiratory distress syndrome.* Review. *Lancet Respiratory Medicine.* 2022;10(11):1086-1098. DOI: [10.1016/S2213-2600(22)00058-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-2600(22)00058-3); PMCID `PMC9423770`. Accessed 2026-09-16; no correction identified in this pass.
  - Exact location: abstract; discusses SpO2/FiO2 as an oxygenation index and identifies reduced accuracy with poor perfusion, at SpO2 above 97%, and in some patients with darker skin pigmentation.
  - Permitted paraphrase: pulse oximetry must be interpreted with FiO2 and known measurement limits; it is not a standalone complete oxygenation assessment.
- Goodfellow et al., AARC PVA CPG, 2024, DOI above.
  - Exact location: p.1044 Figure 1 (oxygenation, ventilation, and hemodynamics as separate physiologic-data components); p.1045 recommendation 6/implementation considerations (assess FiO2 and oxygenation; suggestion, very low certainty).
- Collins J-A, Rudenski A, Gibson J, Howard L, O'Driscoll R. *Relating oxygen partial pressure, saturation and content: the haemoglobin-oxygen dissociation curve.* Physiology review. *Breathe.* 2015;11(3):194-201. DOI: [10.1183/20734735.001415](https://doi.org/10.1183/20734735.001415); PMID `26632351`; PMCID `PMC4666443`. Accessed 2026-09-16; no correction identified in this pass.
  - Exact location: Introduction and **Understanding oxygen saturation and partial pressure**, especially pp.194-196 / PMCID lines 119, 131, 148, 151, and 169. The article identifies saturation, oxygen content, haemoglobin concentration, and cardiac output/distribution as distinct determinants relevant to tissue oxygen delivery.
  - Permitted paraphrase: oxygen saturation is not synonymous with arterial oxygen content or total tissue oxygen delivery; haemoglobin and circulatory factors are additional relevant determinants.

## Applicability and provenance

- Wick concerns ARDS diagnosis/management; M02 uses only its narrower SpO2-with-FiO2 interpretation limit and does not teach ARDS classification, SpO2/FiO2 thresholds, or ARDS management.
- Neither source supports calling FiO2 0.30 or 0.70 good, bad, mild, severe, or an escalation trigger. Those values are authored synthetic case facts.
- Collins supports the limited reason for naming hemoglobin/content/perfusion as downstream unresolved context. It does not authorize M02 to calculate oxygen content/delivery, manage haemodynamics, reason about transfusion, or teach tissue-oxygenation targets.
- Classification: SpO2 is observed display evidence; FiO2 is set support; transfer values are synthetic authored facts.
