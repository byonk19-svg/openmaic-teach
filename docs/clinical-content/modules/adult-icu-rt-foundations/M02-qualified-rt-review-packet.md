### Declared calculations and boundaries

| Item | Declared fact/model | Learner may infer | Learner must not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pattern A | `0.50 L × 20/min = 10.0 L/min`; under stated 0.15 L assumption, `(0.50 − 0.15) × 20 = 7.0 L/min`. | The modeled comparison distinguishes total from modeled alveolar ventilation. | A patient dead-space measurement, normal value, PaCO2, diagnosis, severity, or treatment. |
| Pattern B | `0.25 L × 40/min = 10.0 L/min`; under same assumption, `(0.25 − 0.15) × 40 = 4.0 L/min`. | Same total gas moved can differ in modeled exchange-reaching ventilation. | That rapid/shallow breathing alone proves a real patient outcome. |
| Case B | Reliable SpO2 94%, FiO2 0.70, current matched arterial PaCO2 42 mm Hg, exhaled minute volume 10.0 L/min, patient report “Breathing feels hard right now,” and neck-muscle recruitment. | Each datum addresses a different bounded question; report/observation deserve attention. | Globally reassuring status, complete oxygen delivery, dead-space estimate, quantitative effort, diagnosis, treatment, or acid-base analysis. |
| Transfer | Reliable SpO2 94% on FiO2 0.30 versus reliable SpO2 94% on FiO2 0.70; CO2/effort controlled. | Same output has different support context. | Threshold, severity category, modality choice, escalation, or oxygen-delivery conclusion. |

## 3. Questions for the qualified adult ICU RT reviewer

1. Is the central shortcut—global reassurance from SpO2, PaCO2, and minute ventilation—realistic and worth correcting for experienced ICU RT reasoning?
2. Are the oxygenation, effective-CO2, and breathing demand/effort-related-evidence distinctions physiologically accurate without falsely implying three exhaustive or independent systems?
3. Does the three-pathway visual accurately name hemoglobin/content/perfusion as downstream questions without over-teaching oxygen delivery?
4. Is the fixed 0.15 L non-gas-exchanging teaching assumption acceptably bounded and visibly distinct from a real patient measurement or normal value?
5. Does the paired-breath visual risk a false rule about rapid/shallow breathing, dead space, PaCO2, or treatment despite its model labels?
6. Is current matched PaCO2 42 mm Hg described narrowly enough—as a measured arterial CO2 value under stated conditions—without implying globally adequate ventilation or pulling M03 acid-base analysis into M02?
7. Is SpO2 94% on FiO2 0.70 a fair independent evidence configuration without becoming an implicit treatment, severity, or modality judgment?
8. Are patient report and visible inspiratory neck-muscle recruitment represented as meaningful qualitative evidence without quantifying work, proving a mechanism, or imposing a particular response?
9. Does the output-versus-burden bridge keep drive, muscle effort, work of breathing, dyspnoea, and bedside observations sufficiently distinct?
10. Do the Case B cards reveal provenance only—not the physiological categorization task—and is the independent prompt answerable from visible evidence alone?
11. Does the same-SpO2/different-FiO2 transfer teach support context without stealing M04's modality/trajectory decisions or visually declaring either condition safe/unsafe?
12. Is any material adult ICU RT bedside nuance missing that makes a visual, feedback rule, or synthetic case misleading?
