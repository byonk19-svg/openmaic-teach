## Source-set limitations and concern requiring review

John, Ozanne, and Ho, *Journal of Emergency and Critical Care Medicine* 2021;5:30, DOI `10.21037/jeccm-21-7`, is verified and added to M02-C03 as supplementary letter-to-the-editor physiology context. It does not supply a universal PaCO2 adequacy target, replace stronger primary/authoritative physiology evidence, or justify interpreting PaCO2 42 mm Hg as globally adequate ventilation.

No record establishes a universal adequate PaCO2, oxygenation, effort, or FiO2 threshold. The contract must retain qualified phrasing and its no-treatment/no-modality boundary. The source map supports review readiness, not source closure for future learner-facing copy beyond these stated claims.
