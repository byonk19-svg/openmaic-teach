# Adult ICU RT Foundations roadmap

> State: Generated draft roadmap. This is not clinical-ready instructional content.

## Series boundary

This is a U.S.-baseline, adult acute/ICU RT clinician refresher. It assumes baseline ventilator and ABG literacy. Each module must remain RT-centered while incorporating RT-relevant ICU context only when it changes respiratory assessment, support, airway safety, escalation, or communication.

No roadmap title, objective, or example is approved as a clinical claim. Each built module must independently meet the clinician content standard and complete qualified RT review.

## Proposed sequence

| Order | Generated-draft module | Intended reasoning focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What do we actually know? Patient, support, and respiratory evidence | Separate observations, settings, measurements, calculations, and assumptions; identify consequential uncertainty. |
| 2 | Oxygenation, ventilation, and respiratory demand | Explain which part of the oxygenation/ventilation pathway available evidence can and cannot establish. |
| 3 | ABGs and acid-base without shortcuts | Interpret primary processes, compensation, trends, and sample comparability without treating a formula as a treatment rule. |
| 4 | Oxygen, HFNC, CPAP, and NIV: purpose and trajectory | Relate support purpose to physiology and recognize an uncertain or concerning trajectory. |
| 5 | How an invasive breath is delivered | Interpret trigger, target, cycling, delivery, and measurement conditions without relying on mode labels alone. |
| 6 | Waveforms and patient-ventilator interaction | Describe visible mismatch, alternatives, uncertainty, and relevant patient context. |
| 7 | Airway, circuit, humidification, and delivery safety | Separate patient, airway, circuit, and device concerns; communicate immediate safety priorities. |
| 8 | Lung protection, ARDS, positioning, and heart-lung tradeoffs | Evaluate a respiratory-support change across gas exchange, mechanics, and circulation. |
| 9 | ICU factors that change respiratory reasoning | Recognize sedation, infection, metabolic, renal, nutritional, and goals-of-care context requiring coordinated assessment. |
| 10 | Liberation and integrated bedside reasoning | Separate readiness, trial interpretation, airway questions, post-extubation planning, and unresolved decisions. |

Communication, patient goals, local-policy limits, and escalation framing belong throughout the series rather than only in the final module.

## Build order

Build and review Module 01 completely before authoring production assets for later modules. This validates source records, dependent-asset traceability, qualified RT review, original-visual policy, and release labeling on the smallest useful slice.
