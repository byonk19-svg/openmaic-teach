# U.S. clinician-content evidence baseline

The clinician track uses current U.S. adult-ICU guidance as its primary evidence baseline. Material international guidance differences are recorded in the relevant source record rather than silently averaged into a new recommendation. Local hospital policy remains controlling for bedside practice.

This preserves a coherent default for an RT-first U.S. product while acknowledging that source applicability, local equipment, scope, and policy can differ. It does not authorize patient-specific advice or permit a lesson to override local policy.
