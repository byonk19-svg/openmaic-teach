# OpenMAIC Teach

OpenMAIC Teach is a private, respiratory-therapy-first learning product built on OpenMAIC. It exists to help learners form durable respiratory reasoning, rather than to maximize generated course volume.

## Product

**Clinician track**:
Advanced learning for practicing adult acute/ICU respiratory therapists, centered on bedside interpretation, measurement validity, and transfer to clinical decisions.
_Avoid_: Professional mode, expert course

**Licensure track**:
Scaffolded respiratory fundamentals and RRT licensure preparation for respiratory-therapy students. It prepares learners for the exam but never promises an exam outcome.
_Avoid_: Student mode, guaranteed pass program

**Learning arc**:
The shared instructional sequence of clear visual explanation, worked example, learner retrieval or decision, targeted feedback, and spaced practice or transfer.
_Avoid_: Content flow, quiz loop

**Bedside reasoning**:
The clinician learner's interpretation of observed, measured, set, calculated, and modeled respiratory information before an intervention or conclusion is revealed.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis reveal, answer-first teaching

## Content quality

**Evidence traceability**:
The ability to connect content and objectives to current guidelines, authoritative textbooks, or exam-blueprint-aligned objectives, including applicable dates or versions.
_Avoid_: Evidence-based claim without a source path

**Clinical-ready content**:
Source-traceable learning content that has received qualified RT review before being labeled ready for use.
_Avoid_: AI-approved content, medically certified content

**Generated draft**:
AI-authored learning material that may support exploration or authoring but has not yet earned the clinical-ready label.
_Avoid_: Final content, validated content

## Learning state

**Reasoning checkpoint**:
A learner response point that requires case-specific interpretation, mechanism, and next-step reasoning before its explanation is unlocked.
_Avoid_: Trivia quiz, acknowledgement gate

**Generation completion**:
The state in which every planned scene has been persisted according to the authoritative outline.
_Avoid_: Learner completion, course complete

**Learner completion**:
The state in which every required reasoning checkpoint has a durable passing review.
_Avoid_: Generated course, rendered course
